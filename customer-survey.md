The purpose of this survey is to understand customer preferences, skincare purchasing habits and factors that influence buying decisions.

## 3. Survey Method

- Research Method: Online questionnaire
- Target Respondents: Skincare consumers
- Suggested Sample Size: 20–30 respondents
- Data Collection: Google Forms

## 4. Expected Outcome

The survey will help identify customer preferences, purchasing behaviour, popular digital platforms and opportunities for skincare businesses.
