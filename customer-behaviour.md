
# Customer Behaviour Analysis – Skincare Products

## 1. Introduction
Customer behaviour refers to the decisions and actions people take when selecting, purchasing and using products.

Understanding customer behaviour helps businesses identify customer needs and improve their marketing strategies.

## 2. Factors Affecting Buying Decisions

### A. Product Quality
Customers often consider product quality, ingredients and product information before purchasing.

### B. Price
Affordable pricing and discounts can influence purchasing decisions.

### C. Brand Reputation
Customers may prefer brands that have a positive reputation and provide reliable information.

### D. Customer Reviews
Online ratings, reviews and customer experiences can influence buying decisions.

### E. Advertising
Social media advertisements and influencer promotions can introduce customers to new skincare products.

## 3. Customer Buying Journey

1. Awareness – Customer discovers a skincare product.
2. Interest – Customer searches for product information.
3. Consideration – Customer compares brands and prices.
4. Purchase – Customer decides to buy the product.
5. Post-Purchase – Customer shares feedback or reviews.

## 4. Digital Platforms Influencing Customers

- Instagram
- YouTube
- Google Search
- E-commerce websites
- Beauty and skincare blogs

## 5. Customer Expectations

- Clear product information
- Reasonable pricing
- Convenient online shopping
- Reliable customer reviews
- Good customer support

## 6. Conclusion

Understanding customer behaviour helps skincare businesses develop customer-focused products, improve customer satisfaction and create effective digital marketing campaigns.
