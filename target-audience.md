
# Target Audience Analysis

## 1. Introduction
Target audience refers to the specific group of consumers a business wants to reach through its products and marketing activities.

## 2. Demographic Segmentation
- Age Group: 18–30 years
- Location: Urban and semi-urban areas of India
- Gender: All genders
- Income: Students and working professionals

## 3. Customer Interests
- Skincare and personal care
- Beauty and wellness
- Product ingredients
- Affordable skincare products
- Skin health education

## 4. Customer Needs
- Quality products
- Affordable pricing
- Genuine product information
- Easy availability
- Safe and reliable products

## 5. Online Behaviour
Potential customers may discover skincare products through:
- Instagram
- YouTube
- Google Search
- Beauty blogs
- Influencer recommendations

## 6. Conclusion
Understanding the target audience helps businesses create suitable products, communicate effectively and develop better marketing strategies.
